# BrightTV-Case-Study
# 📺 Bright TV — Viewership & User Analytics Case Study

> **Turning television viewership data into actionable business insights.**

---

## 📌 About the Project

This case study analyses **Bright TV's viewership and user data** to understand audience behaviour, engagement patterns, and user growth over time.

The project follows a data-driven approach to transform raw data into **clear insights and practical recommendations** that can help Bright TV make smarter decisions around content planning, scheduling, and audience engagement.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions:

* 👥 Who are Bright TV's viewers?
* 📺 What are the main viewership patterns?
* ⏰ When are viewers most engaged?
* 📅 How do weekday and weekend viewing patterns differ?
* 📈 How have monthly active users changed over time?
* 💡 How can Bright TV use these insights to improve content planning?

## 📊 Analysis Areas

### 👥 User Profiles

Analysed user information to understand the characteristics and behaviour of Bright TV's audience.

### 📺 Viewership Patterns

Explored viewing activity across different days and times to identify periods of high audience engagement.

### 📅 Weekday vs Weekend

Compared viewing behaviour between weekdays and weekends to determine when audience engagement begins and peaks.

### 📈 Monthly Active Users

Tracked monthly active users to identify audience growth and changes in engagement over time.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis identified important trends around:

* 🕐 **Peak viewing times**
* 📅 **Weekday vs weekend engagement**
* 📈 **Monthly active user growth**
* 👥 **Audience behaviour**
* 📺 **Content engagement opportunities**

These insights provide a clearer understanding of **when and how Bright TV's audience engages with its content.**

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the findings, Bright TV can:

* **Optimise content scheduling** around peak viewing periods.
* **Use weekday and weekend patterns** to plan content more effectively.
* **Monitor monthly active users** to track audience growth.
* **Prioritise high-engagement periods** for important programming.
* **Continue using data-driven insights** to improve audience engagement.
